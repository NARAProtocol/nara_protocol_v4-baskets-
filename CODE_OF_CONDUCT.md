# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work,
and make collaboration possible across different levels of experience.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- protecting security reports and personal information;
- welcoming good-faith questions;
- disagreeing without harassment or personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of credentials, public disclosure of an
unresolved vulnerability, market manipulation, pressure toward a financial
action, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **security@naraprotocol.pro**. Include links
or screenshots when useful, but do not send unnecessary personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project.
